## C. Extension (positive results)

28. **R** (DA M1, ext. 1; DOM ext. 1; EIC E1; METH ext.; PERSP ext. 1): prove a positive result. **A** Three new proved results:
   - **Prop. 6.1** (normalised limits in the trap family): C3C1/C2^2 -> (3/2)(1 - r) and C4C1^2/C2^3 -> 3(1 - 3r + r^2) >= -15/4, with r = V/U.
   - **Prop. 6.2** (variance normalisation does not restore a bound): a six-state product network has both normalised ratios -> -infinity at fixed energies and temperatures. The proposed "repaired" conjecture is therefore false in general.
   - **Thm 6.3** (kinetic lower bound): |C_n| <= n! (g/2) (E_max/l)^n with l = log(1 + g/(2A)), via Bauer-Fike, Riesz projection and Cauchy estimates, with a purely kinetic A for reversible summed chains. Along both families it is of order eps^-2, the true divergence rate; its constants are about 10^4 too large.
